Privacy Policy

Catholic Prayers (com.jcdc.catholicprayers)

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Catholic Prayers mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") created by the developer. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our Application.

Summary: The Application does not require an account or collect personal information such as your name, email, address, or location. Prayers and devotions are stored locally on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to use any feature of the Application.

All core prayer contents, novenas, and litanies are stored locally on your device and are accessible completely offline.

2. Third-Party Services

To improve app performance, stability, and reliability, the Application utilizes Google Firebase services. These services may collect certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage information automatically:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash logs and diagnostic stack traces to help us identify, debug, and fix unexpected errors.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to collect anonymous aggregated usage metrics (such as general device model, OS version, and app performance metrics) to help us understand how features are used and improve user experience.

For more information on how Google handles data, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

The Application requires minimal standard system permissions to function properly. We do not request access to:

  • Precise or Approximate Location
  • Camera or Microphone
  • Contacts or Call Logs
  • Photos, Media, or External Files

4. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our own servers, we do not store, retain, or share personal user data. Data gathered via Firebase Crashlytics is retained in accordance with Google Firebase's standard retention policies solely for troubleshooting purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 knowingly, nor does it collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions regarding your child's use of the app, please feel free to reach out to us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage users to review this page occasionally for any updates.
